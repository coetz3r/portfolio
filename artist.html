Artwork

Art &
Creativity

Blending classical mastery with psychological depth, my artistic work bridges traditional oil painting techniques and surrealist imagination. Drawing deep technical inspiration from Leonardo da Vinci, I approach the canvas with meticulous attention to form, light, and the subtle nuances of atmospheric depth. This foundation is transformed through a surrealist lens reminiscent of Salvador Dalí, allowing realistic textures and figurative precision to dissolve into dreamlike, thought-provoking compositions. Working in oils allows for rich layering and smooth tonal transitions, creating a tangible sense of realism within scenes that defy ordinary logic. Each piece serves as a visual exploration of perception, subverting familiar structures to invite viewers into an immersive, otherworldly narrative.
