Analizing Phase

Interactive Mechanical &
Kinematic Simulation

Overview: A web-first interactive 3D showcase designed to simulate mechanical assemblies and industrial concepts in real time without heavy plugins or video renders.

Execution: Modeled and textured clean assets in Blender, then optimized and rendered via Three.js with baked lighting maps, smooth OrbitControls, and custom animation loops.

Tech Stack: Three.js, WebGL, JavaScript (ES6+), GLSL Shaders, Blender.

Technical Capabilities

Performance &
Asset Pipeline

  • Real-time WebGL Rendering: Lightweight 3D model delivery optimized for smooth 60fps browser performance.
  • CAD-to-Web Pipeline: Streamlined asset workflow from Blender geometry to web-ready GLTF/GLB formats.
  • Interactive Controls: Intuitive rotation, zooming, explosion views, and camera state transitions.
  • Baked Lighting & Shading: Optimized ambient occlusion and realistic surface materials for quick render times.
