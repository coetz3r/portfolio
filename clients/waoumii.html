Overview

Systems Audit &
Refactoring

The original site, a 2014 Wix build, treated every piece of information as equally important — five sub-businesses flattened into one three-level navigation, testimonials marked up as page headings, no single call to action anywhere on the page. There was no hierarchy telling a visitor what mattered most or what to do next.

The redesign starts from the opposite premise: one clear goal per page. The homepage's function is to build trust fast and get a visitor to one action — get a quote — with everything else (the four divisions, the trust signals, the contact info) supporting that single goal rather than competing with it.

Analizing Phase

Initialisation &
Commencement

Every successful project begins with an intensive alignment phase. I deep-dive into your operational ecosystem, target demographic, and core commercial objectives. By auditing existing brand assets and asking deliberate, targeted questions, we uncover the exact technical and creative parameters required to build a layout that solves real business problems.

Once the parameters are clear, I translate that research into a strategic creative direction — a framework that dictates every visual decision before a single pixel is placed, so the design has a reason behind it rather than a vibe.

From there, I move into focused concept development. Rather than presenting scattershot options, I build a small number of strong directions, each backed by the reasoning that ties it back to your original objectives.


This turns feedback into a structured conversation instead of a guessing game.


The result isn't just a layout that looks finished — it's one that's been pressure-tested against the business problem we identified on day one, refined through iteration, and built to scale as your brand grows.

Research & Discovery

Discovery &
System Analysis

Design should never happen in a vacuum. I conduct an architectural analysis of your immediate market competitors to identify visual opportunities and positioning gaps. This research is translated into a curated visual moodboard—establishing the typographic direction, contrast metrics, and geometric style parameters before a single line of vector production begins.

Moving from theory to execution, I build out tailored, mathematically sound design variants. Rather than delivering arbitrary choices, every concept is rigidly evaluated against proven UI/UX and print production principles. When presented, each layout variation is backed by clear technical reasoning, demonstrating exactly how the spatial choices, color systems, and typography serve your brand's authority.

Refine & re-design

Iterative Refinement &
Stress-Testing

Collaboration shapes the final high-performance asset. Using your direct feedback, we systematically refine the chosen direction to lock in absolute visual perfection. Two comprehensive rounds of revision are structurally built into the package pipeline, ensuring the final layout is thoroughly polished, scrutinized, and perfectly tuned to your expectations.

Conclusion & Completion

Technical Handover &
Implementation

A design is only as good as its deployment. Upon final approval, you receive a complete, production-ready asset delivery matrix containing all necessary file formats for digital and commercial press usage (SVG, PNG, and high-resolution print vectors). This is accompanied by an integrated foundational style guide to ensure your brand's typography, colors, and margins remain pristine and consistent for years to come.
