    About Us

    ​​

    Gordon’s Bay Security opened its doors on 1 August 2001 under the leadership of Owner and CEO, Okkie van der Merwe. Since then, we have grown into a trusted name in the security industry and a force to be reckoned with in our community.

    With over two decades of experience, we are proud to offer a comprehensive range of professional security services, delivered with the highest levels of competence, qualification, and expertise. Our commitment to excellence and rapid response ensures that your safety and peace of mind are always our top priority.

    ​

    Armed Response You Can Rely On

    Our team of Armed Response Officers is a formidable and highly trained unit, dedicated to protecting what matters most to you.

    When our fully equipped control room receives an alarm signal from your premises, a Response Officer is immediately dispatched to investigate, inspect, and secure the property. Each officer is rigorously trained and equipped with the knowledge and skills required to handle emergencies and break-ins swiftly and effectively. Their professionalism and readiness ensure that every situation is managed with confidence and care.

    ​

    Expert Technical Support

    Our technical teams are among the best-qualified and most approachable professionals in the security industry. Whether installing new alarm systems or troubleshooting existing ones, our technicians are committed to delivering efficient, reliable service.

    All our staff members are registered with the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSIRA), the governing body of the security industry in South Africa. This registration ensures that our services meet strict industry standards and provides our clients with added assurance and credibility.

    From new installations to resolving enquiries and technical challenges on most alarm systems, our team is always ready to assist with expertise and professionalism.

    ​

    ​

    At Gordon’s Bay Security, your safety is our mission. With experienced leadership, highly trained response teams, and skilled technical professionals, we are committed to protecting your home, business, and community—today and into the future.
